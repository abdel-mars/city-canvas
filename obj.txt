City Lines Art is our own e-commerce studio selling original minimalist city-map prints. We are building an internal publishing tool for our own Pinterest business account. After the owner authorizes the app via OAuth, the tool reads the owner's board list so we can choose one destination board, then creates Pins on that board at scheduled times. Each Pin contains one of our original artwork images, a title, description, alt text, and a link to the matching City Lines product page. The app does not access or store other Pinterest users' data, does not post on behalf of third parties, and does not use Pinterest data for advertising or resale.
